# Issue #4906: [P2 refactor] Relocate LatticeBoundaryBED's anonymous inducedGraph instances to Core.lean (eliminates ~330-site fragility constructively)

**Status:** OPEN

**Created:** 2026-08-04

GitHub: https://github.com/phasetr/ising-model/issues/4906

## Summary

Relocate the two anonymous instances in
`IsingModel/Concrete/LatticeGraphBED/LatticeBoundaryBED.lean:95,107`
(`DecidableRel (Ambient.inducedGraph (latticeGraph d) Λ).Adj` and
`Fintype (Ambient.inducedGraph (latticeGraph d) Λ).edgeSet`) to their definition site
`IsingModel/AmbientLattice/Defs/Core.lean:38` (where `Ambient.inducedGraph` itself is defined),
generalized over an arbitrary `SimpleGraph`, and delete the two lattice-specific anonymous
instances from `LatticeBoundaryBED.lean`.

Supersedes #4897 (closed not-planned — that issue's 28-module Peierls/Conditioning scope was a
mis-targeted ~8-18% slice of a much larger, repo-wide population of 330 loose / 33 strict).

## Why

`dev-design` established (grep-verified on `3fe81b47`) that 330 modules repo-wide (loose
measure; 33 strict) call `inducedGraph (latticeGraph d) Λ`-shaped terms without a direct import
of `LatticeBoundaryBED`, concentrated mostly in `Concrete/` (273+). The defect is
**instance-location**, not naming: moving the two instances to `inducedGraph`'s own definition
site means any module that can even name `inducedGraph` must import `Core`, eliminating the
fragility for all 330 call sites with **zero new import edges**.

build-cost measurement (instances in `Core` are elaborated everywhere).

Use `lake exe shake` to enumerate the exact fragile set (stronger oracle than the grep proxy
used for scoping). Unverified: confirm shake's reporting semantics for anonymous-instance
