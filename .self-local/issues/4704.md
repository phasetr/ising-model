  permissive (stale siblings like `PosReal.lean` match unrelated same-named files) — a

  paragraph with a re-export cue word (`re-exported`/`legacy`/`split into`/`former split`/
  `merged into`/`now lives in`/`lives in`/`live in`) between occurrences. Requires Verbatim
  line-wrap joining as a prerequisite. Comparing against base auto-excludes pre-existing
  occurrences.
