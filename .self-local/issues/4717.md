`IsingModel/Concrete/LatticeGraphCorrelation/Lemma_17_5_2/FiniteRegionPseudoMassDistFV.lean:106`)
and `finiteRegionPseudoMassDistFV_le_pair` (private,

## Scope note

`isDefEq` performance fix) and is out of scope for that PR. Deduplicating the two declarations
(e.g. deleting one and redirecting call sites, or extracting a shared lemma) is a design
decision — it requires choosing the canonical declaration, updating consumers, and confirming
no other near-duplicates exist in the same family.

declaration and update call sites, then verify with a full `lake build`. Per issue #4715's

(`GlobalPseudoMassDistCubicInfFV.lean` already imports `FiniteRegionPseudoMassDistFV`, so the
dedup adds no import).
