# Issue #4721: build-speed: replace cast-positivity with Nat.cast_nonneg at the two FV ratio modules (A/B measured -3.75s)

**Status**: OPEN

**Created**: 2026-07-26 (`dev-perf` A/B measurement, filed via `dev-pr-clerk`)

GitHub: https://github.com/phasetr/ising-model/issues/4721

## Summary

`dev-perf` measured (A/B, own-cost = real − import, median of 3, warm, serial, on a `$TMPDIR`
copy; repo left unmodified during measurement) that 5 `by positivity` call sites across 2 files
are interpreted-`positivity` hot spots and should be replaced with an explicit
`Nat.cast_nonneg _` term.

## Measured

| File | Sites | base own-cost | explicit own-cost | reduction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `IsingModel/Concrete/LatticeGraphCorrelation/Lemma_17_5_2/MassContinuityFiniteVolumeDartRatio.lean:95` | `by positivity` ×3 | 3.73s | 1.61s | −2.12s (−57%) |
| `IsingModel/Concrete/LatticeGraphCorrelation/Lemma_17_5_2/MassContinuityFiniteVolumeIncidentRatio.lean:80` | `by positivity` ×2 | 3.25s | 1.63s | −1.62s (−50%) |
| **Total** | 5 sites | | | **−3.75s** |

Note: `MassContinuityFiniteVolumeDartRatio.lean:95` was originally estimated at 2 `positivity`
calls; the actual count is **3** (all three on the same line, the third below the profiler's
100ms display threshold).

## What the goals actually are

All 5 sites are arguments to `pseudoMassG_ratio_le` / `pseudoMassG_single_ratio_le`'s
non-negativity hypotheses, and reduce to `0 ≤ ((latticeDistance d _ _ : ℕ) : ℝ)` — plain
ℕ→ℝ cast non-negativity, closed by `Nat.cast_nonneg _`.

## Notable findings

- Explicit-term replacement is even faster than a `by sorry` stub at the same site (base 3.73s /
  stub 1.64s / explicit 1.61s), i.e. 100% of the available cost is recovered.
- Sibling non-FV files with the textually identical pattern are out of scope (negligible
  own-cost): `MassContinuityDartSum.lean:96` (−0.12s, noise), `MassContinuityIncidentRatio.lean:120`
  (0s).
- General finding: interpreted `positivity` cost varies 10× across files with an identical call;
  per-file profiling (not textual pattern-matching) is required to find real hot spots.

## Acceptance criteria

- Re-measured combined own-cost reduction ≥ 3.0s.
- `lake build` warning-free; `#print axioms` unchanged; `lake exe GKSTest` pass; zero statement
  changes.
