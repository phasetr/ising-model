## Why this exists

`scripts/measure_module_cost.py` and `scripts/test_measure_module_cost.py` were reviewed five
times inside PR #4820. After round 5 a bound was adopted and recorded in that PR's body: **any
further defect found in this harness is filed as an issue rather than repaired inside PR
#4820**, because four consecutive rounds of in-place hardening kept producing new findings in
the machinery the previous round had added.

None of the items below may be worked on inside PR #4820. They need their own branch, their
own pull request and their own independent review.

Line numbers are read at `9467f42f037ef2c36e32843009800968792cf40b` (base `f23fa1e7`), the
head at which the round-5 review was taken. Round 6 (`2c4a877a`) shifts the

## The five items

| id | review label | subject |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Low 5 (`codex` rated blocking) | `run_capture` has no `timeout=`, now on the per-sample path |
| D2 | Low 4 | cross-check substitution half-supported (breadth 32 -> 1) |
| D3 | Low 6 | nothing asserts `payload["schema"]` or `environment.machine_state_at_start` |
| D4 | Low 7 | H4's ordering half unpinned |
| D5 | Med 1, code half | machine-load guard's post-sample endpoint not recorded |

**D1.** `run_capture` (`measure_module_cost.py:501`) calls `subprocess.run` with no `timeout=`.
Round 5 put it on the per-sample path: `machine_state` (line 630) spawns `pmset -g batt` and
`pmset -g therm` before every timed window. The artifact is serialised in a single write at
`main`, line 1158, after the last sample, so one stuck probe costs the whole run's data. The
timed child itself is unaffected (`measure_once` passes `timeout_s`).

**D2.** Round 5 deleted `ProfileParseTest.test_the_committed_samples_satisfy_the_cross_check`
(32 samples) and named two survivors as its substitute. Only
`ProfileParseTest.test_real_output` (`test_measure_module_cost.py:357`) carries the property:
it runs `parse_profile`, which enforces `IMPORT_CONSISTENCY_ABS_TOLERANCE_S`
(`measure_module_cost.py:233`, applied at line 331). `PlausibilityTest.test_accepts_a_real_sample`
(`test_measure_module_cost.py:503`) calls `import_plausibility_problem`
(`measure_module_cost.py:479`) instead -- a different predicate that never reads the phase
table. Verified independently: max |`import` - `phases.import`| over all 32 committed samples
is exactly 0.0 against a 1e-6 tolerance, so the property holds; only breadth dropped.

**D3.** No case reads `payload["schema"]`, `mmc.SCHEMA` (`measure_module_cost.py:208`) or
`environment.machine_state_at_start`. Verified by grep over the whole test module: the only
`machine_state` references are the two round-5 cases at lines 789 and 810, which assert the
function's return shape and the per-sample key. Reverting the schema tag 4 -> 3 leaves all 58
tests green.

**D4.** `module_digests` (`measure_module_cost.py:803`, called from `main` at line 1087) hashes
`read_bytes()` before the first sample; `test_module_digests_pin_the_measured_bytes`
(`test_measure_module_cost.py:940`) pins the byte source only. The ordering (before the first
sample, not after the last) is demonstrated nowhere in a committed case.

**D5.** Design report section 4.3 registers the guard **pre and post per replicate**; section
4.4 discards on failure at either end. `machine_state()` runs once per sample before the timed
window (`measure_once`, `measure_module_cost.py:660`) and never after the child exits. PR
#4820 round 6 narrows the docstring to say so (making the record honest, not complete); taking
the second reading is code and is deferred here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] D1: a stuck probe cannot hang a run; expiry is a recorded value.
- [ ] D2: the import cross-check tolerance is pinned against more than one real report, or its
      single-report evidence base is stated where a reader of the tolerance sees it.
- [ ] D3: reverting the schema tag, or dropping `machine_state_at_start`, turns at least one
      case red.
- [ ] D4: a case pins the digest ordering, not only the byte source.
- [ ] D5: each sample records the guard's inputs at both ends of its timed window.
- [ ] Delivered outside PR #4820, with its own independent review.
