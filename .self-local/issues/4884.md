commits `167ff124`/`4bfe4aeb`/`e5b7675c` are real), and were then **deliberately rolled back

#4826, both timestamped 2026-08-01T04:35Z ("created during a session whose work is being rolled
back at the user's instruction" / "this work is being rolled back at the user's instruction").

state.

This episode has **no durable repo-tracked record**: `docs/index.md` and
`.self-local/issues/INDEX.md` had zero mentions of it prior to this mirror sync; the only record
was an uncommitted auto-memory session file. This gap is why duplicate re-proposals
(#4885/#4886/#4887) were re-filed re-doing the rolled-back work; they are now closed as
duplicates citing this issue and #4823/#4826.
